Hello, I’m Seogyeong!

I am a product designer who starts with people and keeps testing until it feels clear.

Curiosity and Evidence

I start by reading, listening and asking why. For my first case study I moved from a broad scan of smart-healthcare trends to papers, patient communities and interviews, until one real gap was left: stroke survivors practicing fine motor skills alone at home.

Empathy and Details

I design for the moment someone is actually using the product. I test with real users and experts, treat what breaks as information, and keep refining. In RE:HAND, testing with a stroke survivor and an occupational therapist changed the interaction from dragging to snap-based touch.

Clarity and Craft

I like turning complicated things into one clear interaction, such as pressure and direction shaped into a clay pot instead of a medical chart. I care about the type, spacing and motion that make a product feel calm and easy to trust.
