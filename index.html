The process

  1. 01

    Listen

    Interviews, papers and communities. I start from what people say and do.

  2. 02

    Narrow down

    Cluster the findings, name the insight, and choose one path with clear reasons.

  3. 03

    Prototype

    Sketch many ideas, then build the simplest interaction that proves the point.

  4. 04

    Test & refine

    Test with real users and experts, learn what breaks, and iterate.
