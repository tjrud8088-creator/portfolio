01 · Overview

Deft helps stroke survivors see whether their home practice is working

A tablet app where virtual clay responds to every hand movement and personal records show progress over time.

  • Fine motor rehab
  • Biofeedback
  • Usability testing

Lead UX/UI Designer · Solo · Aug–Nov 2026

02 · The problem

Practicing at home, unsure of each movement

Desk research showed that support thins out once survivors leave the hospital. Literature and community posts point to gaps in follow-up, guidance and participation.

03 · Research & insight

Practice stalls exactly where guidance ends

A 64-minute interview and affinity mapping revealed two moments: practicing less with no one to correct posture, and guessing when to use more force.

04 · Define & ideate

A missing reference point, three How-Might-We questions

In the moment · after a session · over weeks. Ideas for each were converged on one concept using three criteria: action fit, observable input, readable feedback.

05 · Feature 01 · Calibration

Set a starting point that fits today

A short readiness check samples finger range, response speed and hold stability, then proposes a touch target, tolerance and starting level.

06 · Feature 02 · Live feedback

Make movement legible while it happens

Virtual clay makes finger movement visible. A text cue and a progress gauge answer every touch, so users can correct themselves in the moment.

07 · Feature 03 · Progress

Show change across sessions

A progress view charts precision, response time, touch persistence and difficulty across sessions, so small gains become visible.

08 · Testing

Two user rounds and one OT review

A left-side setting error, multi-finger drag friction and the limits of touch-only measurement led to a point-touch pivot. Exploratory findings, not clinical validation.

The process

  1. 01

    Listen

    Interviews, papers and communities. I start from what people say and do.

  2. 02

    Narrow down

    Cluster the findings, name the insight, and choose one path with clear reasons.

  3. 03

    Prototype

    Sketch many ideas, then build the simplest interaction that proves the point.

  4. 04

    Test & refine

    Test with real users and experts, learn what breaks, and iterate.
